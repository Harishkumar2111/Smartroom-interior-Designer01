💡 Pro Designer Painting Tips

  • Ceiling Brightness: Paint ceilings 10-20% lighter than wall colors to make rooms feel higher and airy.
  • Accent Wall: Choose the architectural focal wall (behind bed or media console) for deeper contrast tones.
  • Polygon Tool: Use the Polygon Wall Zone tool to click the 4 corners of any wall for crisp, perfectly masked borders.
  • Blend Mode: Set to Multiply or Realistic Tint to preserve brick, plaster, and shadow depth.
